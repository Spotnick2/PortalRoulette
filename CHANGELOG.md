# Changelog

## v1.0.0

Portal Roulette moves to **WoW: Forever**. The TBC Anniversary version is no longer supported (last
release: v0.1.1, tag `v0.1.1-tbc`).

- Rebuilt for Forever (Interface 16001) on the modern client API, with secure, combat-safe casting.
  Each click casts once, whichever key-down setting you use.
- New frosted-glass wheel:
  - capitals in a diamond, Dalaran inside the wheel, Karazhan as a linked satellite;
  - new painted city art;
  - circulating arcane energy, glowing links with light pulses, a glass shimmer.
- Teleport: Dalaran (new in Forever) and Portal: Karazhan through Atiesh.
- The hearthstone sits in the centre (Hearthstone, Crumbling Hearthstone, hearth toys) with its
  cooldown and bind location.
- Reagent counts include the reagent bag and hide with the Reagent Economy perk.
- The cinematic camera is on by default and is restored after a crash. The game UI hides cleanly,
  and invites stay visible.
- Minimap button (LibDBIcon), Addon Compartment entry, and an options page with sound sets and
  volume channel.
- `/pr preview` shows every destination without casting.
- Removed: the TBC destinations (Shattrath, Silvermoon, Exodar, Stonard, Theramore), and the Dark
  Portal and Naaru's Embrace utilities.
